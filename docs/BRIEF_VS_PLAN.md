## 2. Our additions (not required by the brief; keep small)
- 10-30 rows per edge case (our proposal for testability).
- Extra tables: `document_requirement`, `claim_adjuster_assignment` as a separate table (brief gives only the ASN ID format).
- IDs for Customer, Coverage, Incident, ClaimItem, Adjuster, Assessment, Reserve (brief specifies only five).
- Approval authority matrix and actor role on every event (brief lists five actors but no matrix).
- Fraud-indicator score, precision/recall of our engine vs ground_truth, operational metrics.
- Small Streamlit UI built last. Brief says "working solution" and "expose key decisions and exceptions"; the UI is our reading, not a stated deliverable.
- Optional LLM extension (adjuster summary scored against ground_truth). Brief never mentions AI; never part of generation or validation.
- Tooling: Python/stdlib/pytest/ruff, USD, fixed as-of date, guardrails and hooks.

## 3. Open interpretations (a-c SETTLED 2026-10-30, see SPEC "Settled decisions"; d-g stand as assumptions)
Settled: (a) baseline conditions are rule-produced and labelled `origin=baseline`; (b) duplicates share one label,
split by `origin`; (c) starter = scale 0.05, full = scale 1.0.
a. **Step 4 vs Step 5 conditions.** Step 4 puts duplicate claims, outside-coverage incidents, incomplete evidence etc. in the baseline; Step 5 injects six more after a "valid baseline". Our SPEC lists step-4 items as "Also" without labels. Decision needed: treat step-4 conditions as rule-handled business outcomes and also record them in ground_truth (recommended), distinct from the six injected cases.
b. **"Duplicate claims" (step 4) vs "duplicate incident" (step 5).** Likely overlapping; decide whether they share one label or two.
c. **Starter vs full.** Brief says both use the same generator. We assume full = 2,000/1,000 and starter = small `scale`; confirm in README.
d. **ASN.** Brief never defines it; we read it as adjuster assignment, with Assessment as ASM-. Only the prefix changes if wrong.
e. **DOC-<claim-id>-001.** Literal reading gives `DOC-CLM-2026-000001-001`.
f. **1,000 claims include duplicates and anomalies** (assumed, affects counts).
g. **Ground-truth kind.** Brief says "expected decisions or labels"; we add a business-outcome vs integrity-defect column.

## 4. Risks
- Scope creep: UI, metrics and AI extension are extras; the graded part is the data (review points a-g). Finish the core and `/verify` first.
- A label column in an input table, random outcomes, status overwrite, orphan FKs from injection, or clock/unseeded randomness would each fail a stated criterion.
- Brief does not give counts for customers, coverages, adjusters, or per-case anomaly rows; we choose and document them.
